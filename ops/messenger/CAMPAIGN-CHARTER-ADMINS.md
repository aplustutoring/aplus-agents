# Charter admins: separate campaign, SPEC ONLY. Not built.

Roman 2026-08-25: charter admins are **not a fifth tier** of the teacher
campaign. Different audience logic, different ask, and a suppression problem
the teacher campaign does not have.

Nothing in this document has been built. No list, no email, no workflow.

## Why they are not a tier

A Teacher of Record is thinking about one student on their caseload. A charter
admin is thinking about:

* **vendor quality across their whole program**, not one child
* **board optics** and how a vendor choice reads upward
* **justifying enrichment fund spend**, which is the part that gets questioned

An email asking "is there a student you are worried about" is the wrong question
for someone whose job is the portfolio, not the case.

## The ask

Not a student. One of:
* a conversation
* staff visibility (introducing A+ to their teachers, or a slot at a staff
  meeting or PD day)
* placement on the approved vendor list

## Badge language: reused, reframed

The Badge paragraph carries over, and it is stronger here than with teachers,
because independent review of program design is exactly the evidence an admin
needs to justify a vendor choice upward.

**Every hard compliance rule from the teacher campaign still applies**, and one
matters more here: the Badge speaks to **program design, not effectiveness**.
An admin justifying spend to a board is precisely the person who would want to
hear "proven results", and precisely the person we must not say it to.
