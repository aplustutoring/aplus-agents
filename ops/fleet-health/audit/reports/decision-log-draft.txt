DECISION: Marketing-campaign replies are a first-class routing category, and the
  LOCKED routing table is amended to carry them. Two categories: campaign_family
  (a family replying to an announcement or marketing send — sign-ups, added
  sessions, referrals) owned by charter_sales at a 90-minute SLA, and
  campaign_school (a TOR/EF/ES, coordinator or school leader replying) owned by
  sales at 8 business hours. Both draft a reply. school_partner outranks
  campaign_school whenever the email is really program, contract or PO business.
  Classification requires POSITIVE EVIDENCE in the email itself — quoted campaign
  text, the campaign subject line, or an explicit mention of its subject matter.
  Timing and list membership are explicitly NOT evidence. With no signal present
  the campaign is ignored and the email is classified on its content.
  rules.md carries an "Active campaigns" block, maintained per campaign and
  retired when replies stop.
WHY: The NSSA Badge announcement (3 segmented sends, 7,268 recipients,
  2026-08-31 and 09-01) used reply-as-CTA, so every response landed in the
  agent-triaged admin inbox with no category that fit. Without the categories,
  family sign-ups would have routed to the schedulers instead of charter_sales.
  The evidence rule is the correction: on day one the agent tagged four replies
  as campaign traffic and two were false positives (Danielle confirmed Erica
  Porter's was ordinary tutoring correspondence; Jaclyn Bershadsky's predated the
  leads send). The first version listed the positive signals but never said
  timing plus list membership was insufficient, so the classifier filled the gap
  itself. Misrouting a real request into a courtesy lane costs more than missing
  a congratulations note.
  Verified against the live classifier, not asserted: both false positives now
  return unknown for human review, while the one genuine Badge reply stays
  campaign_school with confidence rising 0.82 to 0.92.
EVIDENCE: PR #134 (categories, merged 2026-08-31), PR #159 (evidence rule).
  Campaign result: 40.0% open on the school list, 14.3% on active families,
  unsubscribes 1.6%, spam effectively zero, one same-day family referral.
