# Teacher Scholarship Program — canonical description

programme changes, change it HERE first, then propagate.

Owner: Danielle (Director of School Partnerships).

## What it is

> **A teacher nominates a student. The student receives two 45-minute sessions
> with one of our tutors: one assessment, one instruction.**

- **Two 45-minute sessions per nominated student.** The first sees where the
  student actually is. The second teaches. Say both, because "a free session"
  undersells it and "free tutoring" oversells it.
- **Two nominations per teacher.**
- **The ask per nomination is small on purpose:** the student's name, and one
  sentence on why them.

## Overflow: the cap is a starting point, not policy

**If a teacher says they have more than two students, the answer is yes.**

Roman 2026-08-25: do not let a high-intent responder get a no. A teacher who
comes back with four names is the single best outcome this programme can
produce, and refusing two of them to defend a number we invented would be
self-defeating.

Danielle must know this before the campaign sends. It is not a rule to be
mentioned in the copy — the copy says two, so the ask stays small and specific —
but it is honoured on request without hesitation or negotiation.

## Approved language

Short form (P.S., SMS, a line in a longer email):

> Teacher Scholarship Program: nominate a student and tell me why them. They get
> two 45-minute sessions with one of our tutors, one to see where they are and
> one to teach. You can nominate two students.

Never say: "one free session", "covers one student in full", "fully funded",
"a free tutoring spot", "free one-on-one tutoring matched to where they need
support", or anything implying ongoing tutoring.

Never imply the teacher's nomination is refused. Never imply it is automatic
either — see the selection note below.

## Selection

Criteria: the student's need, and the teacher's rationale for putting them
forward. Both are real and both are read.

**In practice nominations are not rejected**, and the overflow rule above means
extra nominations are honoured too. So copy must NOT assert rejection —
"we select from the nominations", "limited spots", "not every nomination is
accepted". A teacher who nominates and sees every name accepted has caught us
manufacturing scarcity, and teachers at one school compare notes.

Asking for the rationale is what makes the choice feel weighed, and it is the
actual criterion, so it is simply true.

## After the sessions

The intended path is that the family continues using their **instructional
funds** for tutoring. Where a family has none, we look for other avenues.

Copy rules:
- Never present the two sessions as the end of the offer, and never imply
  ongoing free tutoring.
- Do not lead a nomination ask with funding mechanics. The teacher's job is to
  name a student, not to means-test a family.
- Honest answer to "what happens after?": the family can continue with their
  instructional funds, and if they do not have any we will look at options
  with them.
