# Teacher Scholarship Program — canonical description

**This file is the source of truth for how the programme is described.** Every
email, SMS, landing page, agent template and human reply must match it. If the
programme changes, change it HERE first, then propagate.

Owner: Danielle (Director of School Partnerships). Established 2026-08-24 after
the teacher campaign described the offer wrong twice in one day, both times by
reading the marketing emails instead of a definition. There was no definition.

## What it actually is

> **A teacher nominates a student. We select from the nominations. The selected
> student receives one free tutoring session.**

Three parts, and all three matter:

1. **The teacher nominates.** They put a student forward. Up to three students,
   one form submission each.
2. **We select.** Nomination is not acceptance. There is a selection step with
   criteria, run by us.
3. **The award is one free session.** Not a funded place, not a term of
   tutoring, not an ongoing programme.

Scarcity is deliberate: not every nominated student is selected. Say so plainly.
It is honest, and a selective award is worth more to a teacher than a giveaway.

## Approved language

Short form (P.S., SMS, a line in a longer email):

> Teacher Scholarship Program: you nominate a student, we select from the
> nominations, and the student selected gets a free session.

Never say: "covers one student in full", "fully funded", "a free tutoring spot",
"free one-on-one tutoring matched to where they need support", or any phrasing
that implies ongoing tutoring or automatic acceptance.

Never imply the teacher's pick is the decision. They nominate; we select.
