## Report (Roman)

> :pushpin: *How this channel works* — read once, then just use it.
> 
> *What it's for:* anything one of our agents got wrong, missed, or did annoyingly. The blog builder, the spotlight builder, the inbox agent, the call agent, the PO agent — all of them. If it felt broken, it belongs here.
> 
> *How to report:* just say it in plain English, as a new message in the channel. No format, no ticket number, no tagging anyone.
> 
> &gt; _the spotlight for Amelia is missing the superhero reel_
> &gt; _Danielle's op-ed got cut off again on the Visions post_
> &gt; _the call agent assigned the follow-up to the wrong person_
> *Screenshots are welcome.* :warning: They were being silently dropped until today — if you attached one and got no reply, that's why, and it wasn't you. Fixed now. Note the agent reads your _words_, not the image, so add a sentence saying what the screenshot shows.
> 
> *What happens next, usually within two minutes:*
> 1. The agent replies in a thread confirming what it understood
> 2. It files the report so it can't get lost
> 3. It posts a diagnosis and a proposed fix, and asks for a go-ahead
> *Then you decide. Reply in that thread with one word:*
> 
> • `approve` — build the fix. A pull request comes back into the thread.
> • `merge` — ship it. That's the fix live.
> • `no` — leave it filed, don't fix it now.
> *Roman, Danielle, Paola and Emily can all approve and merge.* If you reported it, you can approve and ship it yourself — you don't need to wait for Roman.
> 
> *Other things you can say in a thread:*
> • `accept` — file the report as-is with no code change
> • `close` — drop it
> • Something misbehaving badly? Say so and ask to demote it — that pauses the agent to draft-only until a human reviews. Roman executes those.
> *Two honest limits:*
> • Everything here is staff-only and private. Don't paste student or family details in a report — describe the problem and the agent will keep the specifics in the thread, not in the permanent record.
> • Nothing ships without one of us saying `approve` and `merge`. The agent writes code; it never deploys on its own.
> *If you get no reply at all within a few minutes, something is broken upstream* — tell Roman directly rather than assuming your report landed.
> 
> Every Friday there's a digest of what was reported, grouped by agent, never by person. The scoreboard tracks agents, not people. Nobody is being counted here. *Sent using* <@U0AKFN28V1U>
